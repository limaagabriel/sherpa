# Lens: naive

Owns an unanchored take on the problem, free of precedent and of the obvious framework.

## Proposing

Propose a direction that a newcomer without our habits might see.

- Ignore how it is done today and which framework or tool is the usual answer.
- Find the assumption the problem seems to rely on, drop it, and ask what becomes possible.
- Try the extremes: what if it had to cost $0, and what if the budget were unlimited?
- Prefer a plainly different shape over a variation of the common one; say what it would take to
  make it real.
- A good proposal may look odd on purpose; state which assumption it removed and what that risks.

## Out of scope

- Fit with existing patterns and one-way doors → architecture.
- Edge cases and tests → correctness.
- Failure under misuse → robustness.
- Trimming scope of the obvious solution → minimality.
